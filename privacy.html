Privacy Policy

Effective August 2, 2026 · NTTD LLC

SportRelay is a pitch-calling app made by NTTD LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information we handle when you use the SportRelay mobile app or visit sportrelay.app, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short, because there genuinely isn't much to tell.

The short version: SportRelay has no accounts and no sign-in. Your calls, custom pages, and settings live on your device and are never sent to us. The only data connected to you is what's needed to process your subscription through Apple or Google, handled by our payments partner RevenueCat, plus a launch-time check for app updates (technical metadata only — see Section 2). We don't sell data, we don't run ads, and we don't want information we don't need.

1. Information we do not collect

SportRelay’s pitch-calling and game functions work entirely on your device. Apart from the subscription processing and launch-time update check described in Section 2, we do not collect, transmit, or store on our servers:

  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials — the app has no accounts.
  • Your pitch calls, custom pages, custom buttons, game clock, pitch counts, or settings. These are stored locally on your device only and can be erased at any time with the in-app "Reset all app data" control.
  • Audio. The app speaks through your device's text-to-speech; it does not record, and it never uses your microphone.
  • Your precise location, contacts, photos, or messages.

2. Information handled to run your subscription and keep the app updated

SportRelay is sold as a subscription through the Apple App Store and Google Play. When you purchase or start a trial:

  • Apple or Google processes the payment. We never see your card number or billing details. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat, our subscription-management provider, receives purchase receipt data and assigns your device a random, anonymous identifier so the app can confirm your subscription is active (including offline). This identifier is not your name or email and is not tied to an account. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf; see the RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Expo (EAS Update) — when you open the app, it may check Expo’s update service for improvements to the app’s code and download them in the background. This happens only at launch — never during an active game session — and pitch-calling, text-to-speech, and all game functions remain fully offline. The check transmits technical metadata only: your device’s operating system (iOS or Android), the app’s version/runtime and update channel, and a random installation token. It contains no personal information, no game data, and no account data (none exists). See the Expo privacy policy.

We can see aggregate subscription information (for example, how many active subscribers exist and anonymized transaction records) but we cannot identify you personally from it.

3. Support email

If you email support@sportrelay.app, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to respond to you and for nothing else. We keep support correspondence only as long as useful for helping you.

4. The website

sportrelay.app is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or cookies on it. GitHub may log standard technical information (such as IP addresses) to operate the hosting service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

5. What we share

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers. Information is handled only by the service providers named above (Apple or Google, RevenueCat, Expo, GitHub) as needed to operate the app and site, or if the law genuinely requires us to.

6. Children

SportRelay is built for coaches and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: all app data is yours. Delete it anytime with "Reset all app data" in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Subscription records: receipt and transaction data is retained by Apple/Google and RevenueCat per their policies and as required for accounting.
  • Requests: to ask what data exists about you or to request deletion of anything we control (such as support emails or the anonymous subscription identifier), email support@sportrelay.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

The most private data — your calls and settings — never leaves your device, which is the strongest protection available. The limited subscription data described above is transmitted over encrypted connections.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, and to know what is collected. Because we hold almost nothing tied to you, most of these requests are satisfied by Section 7. We honor valid requests regardless of where you live, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising them. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change meaningfully affects how your information is handled (for example, if a future team feature introduces accounts), we will describe it plainly in the app or on this site before it takes effect.
